摘  要 
I 
摘  要 
类脑计算芯片借鉴人脑信息处理机制，融合现有计算科学技术，为发展人工
通用智能提供了一种高效算力平台。类脑计算芯片架构具有众核分布、高并行
度、低功耗、存算一体等特性，其内部众核之间存在多种同步、异步以及混合机
制，导致将类脑算法映射至类脑芯片存在高度的不确定性。随着类脑算法的多样
性发展，这种不确定性急剧放大，严重制约了类脑芯片发挥其优势。针对这一缺
陷，本文展开了网络资源调度到物理映射与芯片架构之间效率的研究。为提高类
脑众核计算架构计算效率，本文研究了：任意规模神经网络时空资源与有限硬件
时空资源之间由于调度策略所引起的计算延迟；由时空资源调度产生的数据负荷
引起相关的物理映射 NP 问题下的传输延迟；进而通过优化硬件架构实现对计算
时间的加速。本文的主要成果和创新点包括： 
1. 时空资源调度策略：针对单个 core 簇的计算任务，由调度策略产生附加
数据所引起的计算延迟导致芯片计算效率降低的问题，本文提出了非循环式、循
环式、混合式时空资源调度策略模型。通过分析调度策略的空间冗余度、时间延
迟，提出了单任务下的时空映射极限，并且证明了循环式资源调度能够达到单任
务时空映射效率极限。 
2. 物理映射算法：针对单个 core 簇向 2D 平面部署时，由调度策略产生的传
输负荷在物理映射 NP 问题引起的传输延迟导致芯片计算效率降低的问题，对上
述三种时空资源调度模型分别构建三种不同的物理映射算法。提出了 4D 到 2D
分层算法、汉密尔顿回路算法、混合型算法。分析验证了 4D 到 2D 分层算法的
搜索空间小于(4!×2!×10!)，其传输时间能够接近物理映射传输时间极限。通过
理论分析和实验证明了汉密尔顿回路算法、混合型算法能够逼近物理映射极限。 
3. 高效硬件架构设计：针对 MAC 结构、 尺寸， 存算比例设计不合理导致算力
效率降低的问题，分别构建高维 MAC 阵列尺寸与神经网络尺寸匹配效率模型、高
维 MAC 结构与原语效率模型、存算比例与映射效率的模型。 在此基础上，设计两
种尺寸 3D MAC 阵列，对类脑众核架构芯片原语指令、智能信息存储机制、多任
务内存访问机制等进行了优化。最后编写仿真系统对设计的分布众核架构进行验
证。 
 
关键词：计算效率；时空资源调度；映射算法；时空映射效率；分布众核

Abstract 
Brain-inspired computing chips, which draw on the information processing 
mechanisms of the human brain, integrate existing computational science technologies to 
provide an efficient computing platform for the development of artificial general 
intelligence. The architecture of brain -inspired computing chips features multi -core 
distribution, high parallelism, low power consumption, and inte grated storage and 
computation. The internal cores operate using various synchronous, asynchronous, and 
hybrid mechanisms, creating a high level of uncertainty when mapping brain -inspired 
algorithms onto these chips. This uncertainty is exacerbated by the diversity of brain -
inspired algorithms, significantly constraining the chips' ability to leverage their 
advantages. To address this limitation, this paper investigates the efficiency of network 
resource scheduling and physical mapping in chip architecture.  To enhance the 
computational efficiency of brain-inspired multi-core architectures, this study examines: 
1．The computational delays caused by scheduling strategies due to the discrepancy 
between temporal and spatial resources of neural networks of arbitrary scale and finite 
hardware resources.  2. T he transmission delays related to the physical mapping NP 
problem caused by spatial and temporal resource scheduling . 3 Optimization of the 
hardware architecture to accelerate computation time . The main contributions and 
innovations of this paper include: 
1. Temporal-Spatial Resource Scheduling Strategy: To address the issue of reduced 
chip computational efficiency caused by additional data -induced computational delays 
from scheduling strategies for a single core cluster's computational tasks, this paper 
proposes non-cyclic, cyclic, and hybrid temporal-spatial resource scheduling models. By 
analyzing the spatial redundancy and temporal delay of scheduling strategies, the paper 
identifies the limits of temporal -spatial mapping under single -task scenarios and 
demonstrates that cyclic resource scheduling can achieve the op timal efficiency for 
single-task temporal-spatial mapping. 
2. Physical Mapping Algorithm : To tackle the problem of reduced chip 
computational efficiency due to transmission load -induced delays in physical mapping 
NP problems from scheduling strategies for deploying a single core cluster onto a 2D

temporal-spatial resource scheduling models. The paper introduces the 4D-to-2D layered 
algorithm, the Hamiltonian circuit al gorithm, and the hybrid algorithm. It is shown

3. Efficient Hardware Architecture Design : To address the issue of reduced

efficiency, and compute-to-memory ratio with mapping efficiency are constructed. Based 
on these models, two sizes of 3D MAC arrays are designed, and optimizations are made 
for primitive instructions, intelligent information storage mechanisms, and multi -task 
memory access mechanisms in the brain-inspired multi-core architecture chips. Finally, a 
simulation system is developed to validate the designed distributed multi -core 
architecture.
