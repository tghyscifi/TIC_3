摘  要 
I 
摘  要 
将计算机科学和脑科学融合 被公认为发展人工通用智能的最佳方法之一 。因
此，近年来有越来越多的 研究将计算机科学导向的人工神经网络和脑科学导向的
脉冲神经网络融合， 构建优势互补的跨范式类脑计算模型。这种跨范式融合中既需
要支持二者的单独建模， 也需要支持异构混合建模。 但是， 由于二者在信息表示、
计算原理和存储组织 等方面存在显著差异，目前尚无有效支撑二者同时运行的芯
片和硬件平台，极大限制了对混合模型中 多模态数据交互和多网络并行协同 等独
特机制的探索和实现。另一方面， 两种计算范式的算法模型具有多样性，复杂网络
模型对支撑融合建模的硬件平台的执行效率带来了挑战。针对上述问题，本文采用
软硬件协同迭代发展的研究方法，提出了同时支撑人工神经网络和脉冲神经网络
的跨范式混合建模以及高效灵活执行的解决方案， 设计并实现了高度灵活、可编程
的跨范式融合类脑计算芯片。主要研究内容和成果包括： 
（1）针对人工神经网络和脉冲神经网络 具有不同 信息表示和处理方式 而难
以融合的挑战，本文提出了跨范式可重构的众核神经形态芯片，Tianjic Ⅱ，采用统
一的功能核结构实现了对于两种网络计算范式的同时支持。 本方案通过拆解典型
网络模型的计算操作，构建了统一的模型描述框架，并通过多模块功能核的灵活配
置，实现了不同模式的计算组合。 功能核间可扩展的路由连接和多级时序执行机制
有效支持了不同范式网络模型在芯片上的映射与执行。 
（2）针对混合网络模型缺乏硬件部署实现方案的问题，本文提出了面向跨范
式众核芯片的端到端混合网络映射框架和硬件接口平台，实现了对多模态信号交
互和多网络并行协同的良好支撑。本工作总结了典型的信号交互转换方法， 建立了
对应的硬件配置方案，并通过全局的时序调整机制匹配了异构模块间的执行周期。
模块化的外部接口方案解决了多网络并行协同时的控制调度和数据分发问题。 
（3）针对于传统神经形态芯片应对复杂多样的网络模型时可编程性差、资源
利用率低的问题，设计并实现了新一代时空多粒度的类脑计算芯片，Tianjic X1 。
其具有粗粒度原语指令,并且支持功能核间的异步时序簇分组，实现了网络执行的
时空多粒度优化。本方案通过在功能核中引入按照原语组堆栈译码执行的方式， 提
高了其独立可编程能力。 多级异步的时序簇分组机制增强了功能核间执行的负载
均衡，实现对单网络高效紧凑执行以及多网络之间的异步信息交互的高效支撑。 
本文软硬件协同迭代的 思路为发展通用类脑计算平台 提供了值得借鉴的道路。 
关键词： 类脑计算 ；神经形态芯片 ；跨范式融合 ；网络映射 ；时空执行

Abstract 
Integration of computer science and brain science has emerged as one of the best 
approaches to develop artificial general intelligence. Therefore, in recent years, more and 
more research ha s integrated the computer -science-oriented artificial neural network s 
(ANNs) and the brain -science-oriented spiking neural networks (SNNs) to build cross -
paradigm brain -inspired computing models with complementary advantages.  This 
integration needs to support individual modeling of them as well as their hybrid models. 
However, due to the significant differences between ANNs and SNNs in terms of 
information representation, computation principle, and memory organization, there lacks 
an effective chip and hardware platform that can support them running simultaneously. 
This further greatly limits the exploration and implementation of unique mechanisms 
such as multimodal data interaction and multi -network parallel cooperation in hybrid 
models. On the other hand, the algorithms and models of these two computing paradigms 
are d iverse, and the complex network structures bring challenges to  the execution 
efficiency of the cross-paradigm hardware platform. Through the iteration of hardware -
software co -optimization, this work proposes a solution that can support the hybrid 
modeling and efficient execution of ANNs and SNNs, and implements a cross-paradigm 
brain-inspired computing chip with high flexibility and programmability. The main  
research contents and novelties include: 
(1) Regarding the challenge that ANNs and SNNs  is hard to integrate due to their 
different information representation and processing methods , this work proposes a 
reconfigurable cross-paradigm many-core neuromorphic chip, Tianjic Ⅱ, which adopts a 
unified functional core (FCore) to support the two computing paradigms concurrently. By 
dismantling the computing operations in typical network models of the two paradigms, a 
unified hardware implementation framework  is built, in which different combination 
modes of computing operations can be realized through the flexible configuration of the 
multi-module FCores. Further, the scalable routing connection and multi -level timing 
schedule among FCores effectively support the mapping and execution of different 
network models on chip.

摘  要 
III 
(2) Regarding the lack of hardware d eployment scheme for hybrid neural network 
(HNN) models, this work proposes a mapping framework and hardware platform for the 
end-to-end implementation of HNN models on the cross -paradigm neuromorphic chip. 
The proposed solution provides good support for t he multimodal data interaction and 
multiple networks’ parallel collaboration in the hybrid models. Specifically, typical signal 
conversion methods  are summarized and their hardware configuration scheme  is 
established. On this basis , a global timing adjustment mechanism matches the different 
execution periods between heterogeneous modules. A modular external interface solution 
solves the system scheduling and data distribution problems in the parallel multi-network 
cooperation tasks. 
(3) Regarding the problems of poor programmability and low resource utilization in 
traditional neuromorphic chips dealing with complex and diverse network models , this 
work designs and implements a new generation and spatiotemporal multi -granularity 
brain-inspired chip, Tianjic X1. It has coarse-grained primitive instructions and supports 
asynchronous timing cluster grouping among FCores, which realizes the spatiotemporal 
multi-granularity optimization of the neural network execution. By introducing the stack 
sequential execution of multiple primitives to the FCore, its independent programmability 
is greatly improved. The multi -level asynchronous sequential cluster grouping 
mechanism enhances the load balance between FCores, and the efficient and compact 
execution of single network and asynchronous information interaction between multiple 
networks are realized. 
In this work, the method of hardware-software co-optimization provides a new way 
for the development of a general brain-inspired computing platform. 
 
Key words: brain-inspired computing; neuromorphic chips; cross-paradigm integration; 
network mapping; spatiotemporal execution
