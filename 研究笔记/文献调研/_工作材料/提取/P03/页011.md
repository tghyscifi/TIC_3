Hybrid automatic quantization framework for ANN and SNN. A quantization
method based on constraint training is proposed to meet special hardware constraints, and
based on this method, an ANN automatic quantization framework is developed, whose ef-
fectiveness is verified under strict hardware constraints; an SNN automatic quantization
framework supporting different input modes based on the basic operations in the SNN
programming framework is developed, which is more flexible and versatile than quanti-
zation for specific spiking neurons.
Based on the idea of decoupling, the output IR of the brain-inspired high-level com-
piler, Task Graph, is designed, with unified ANN and SNN and separate computation-
storage-access representations. A Transformer that gradually lowers brain-inspired algo-
rithm models into Task Graphs satisfying various hardware constraints through a series
of compilation passes is developed. The Transformer and the Task Graph IR establish the
abstraction and protocol, compilation process, software architecture, and infrastructure at
the core of the brain-inspired high-level compiler.
